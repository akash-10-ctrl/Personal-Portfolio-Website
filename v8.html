CAD PROJECT

V8 ENGINE ASSEMBLY

A detailed V8 engine assembly designed in SolidWorks, featuring pistons, connecting rods, crankshaft and timing components.

SOFTWARE

SolidWorks

TYPE

3D CAD Assembly

STATUS

Completed

01 / OVERVIEW

THE PROJECT

This project focuses on the design and assembly of a V8 engine using SolidWorks. The assembly was created by modelling individual mechanical components and combining them into a complete engine system.

The project helped me understand mechanical assembly, component relationships, mates, motion and the interaction between different engine components.

02 / COMPONENTS

KEY COMPONENTS

Crankshaft

Designed to convert the reciprocating motion of the pistons into rotational motion.

Connecting Rod

Connects the piston to the crankshaft and transfers the forces generated during combustion.

Pistons

Modelled as part of the reciprocating mechanism within the engine assembly.

Timing System

Includes the timing components responsible for synchronising engine operation.

03 / DESIGN PROCESS

FROM PARTS TO ASSEMBLY

01

Part Modelling

Individual engine components were modelled using SolidWorks.

02

Assembly

Components were assembled using appropriate mates and mechanical relationships.

03

Motion Study

The assembly was tested through a SolidWorks motion study to visualise component movement.
